# Requirement: RQ-003

## Title

The built-in edition must appear in the template list, and the template switches
must do what they say

## Description

Every edition is poured into a template. The Templates screen lists them, and
each row offers two switches: **Use this one**, described as applying when a send
does not name a template, and **Preselect in the builder**.

The redesigned edition, built from the AI Radar design and shipped as code rather
than stored HTML, had no row. It was reachable only as an option called "Default
Template": no name, no description, and none of the controls the other templates
had. Someone reviewing the list saw the same seven templates as before and
concluded the new design had not been built.

Worse, one of the two switches did nothing. **Use this one** wrote a flag no code
read, so a send that named no template always used the built-in edition whatever
the screen showed. A row could display "In use" while not being used. **Preselect
in the builder** did work.

Separately, the query that enforced "only one at a time" was not scoped to an
organization, so changing which template one organization used cleared the flag
for every other organization.

The goal is that the list shows every template a send can actually use,
including the built-in one, and that both switches change behaviour.

## Source

Raised by Julian Andrade after reviewing the Templates screen and finding the
same templates as before the redesign.

## Actions

1. **List the built-in edition**: The system must show the built-in edition
   alongside the stored templates, with a name, a description, and the same
   switches.
2. **Mark it as built in**: The system must show that it ships with the
   application and cannot be edited visually or deleted, so nobody looks for
   controls that cannot exist.
3. **Honour the in-use switch**: A send that names no template must use whichever
   template is marked as in use, and the built-in edition when none is.
4. **Honour the preselect switch**: The builder must open on whichever template
   is marked for preselection, and the built-in edition when none is.
5. **Keep one at a time**: Marking a template must unmark the previous one,
   within that organization and no other.
6. **Never present a dead switch**: The system must not offer an interaction that
   cannot take effect, such as switching off the built-in edition when nothing
   else is available to take its place.

## Preconditions

- The organization exists and can hold templates of its own.
- The administrator is authorized to change the organization's templates.

## Postconditions

- The list names every template a send can use.
- Changing a switch changes which template the next send or edition uses.
- One organization's choice does not affect another's.

## Inputs

- **Template choice**: Which template a send or a new edition should use.
- **Stored templates**: Templates authored in the visual builder.

## Outputs

- **Template list**: The built-in edition and the stored templates, each showing
  whether it is in use and whether it is preselected.
- **Rendered newsletter**: Produced by whichever template applies.

## Business Rules

- **BR-001**: A setting the product offers must take effect, or must not be
  offered. (Shared with RQ-002.)
- **BR-002**: A stored preference governs the organization that stored it, and no
  other. (Shared with RQ-002.)
- **BR-007**: At most one template is in use, and at most one is preselected. The
  built-in edition holds each of those positions precisely when no stored
  template does.
- **BR-008**: What ships with the application cannot be edited or deleted from
  within it.

## Dependencies

- The ability to store templates per organization.
- The ability to render an edition without a stored template.

## CRUD Operations

- **CREATE**: None.
- **READ**: The template list, and which template applies to a send.
- **UPDATE**: Which template is in use, and which is preselected.
- **DELETE**: None. The built-in edition cannot be deleted, and this requirement
  removes no stored template.

## Note on the flow

The clarify and specify gates were compressed at the requester's instruction:
the two decisions that would have been asked, whether the built-in appears as a
first-class entry and whether the seeded templates stay, were settled in
conversation before the work began. The seeded templates are untouched.
